# Proposed CNCF Forum collaboration

Independent discussion proposal. No CNCF sponsorship, administrator authorization, pilot participation or endorsement has been obtained or implied.

## Purpose and mutual benefit

This proposal does not assume CNCF needs the intensive moderation contemplated for other communities. It invites interested maintainers and members to evaluate a narrow contributor-opportunity and governance-assistance pilot, if administrators judge it useful.

Potential community benefit: clearer opt-in opportunity discovery, explainable recommendations, open consent and evaluation artifacts, and less effort assessing a proposed tool. Potential project benefit: feedback on usability and permission boundaries, documented failures and reusable governance patterns. These are hypotheses to measure, not guaranteed outcomes.

The longer-term goal is collaboration with communities where dependable professional interaction matters: developer Discord servers, professional associations and authorized Slack channels. Each community makes its own decision. A CNCF pilot does not authorize another deployment, commercial solicitation or use of member data elsewhere.

## Pilot charter for discussion

- Stage A: public GitHub and synthetic evidence only; no community installation.
- Stage B: optional personal read-only pilot with explicit purpose-specific consent.
- Stage C: only after written administrator authorization, a named public-channel allowlist, approved Slack app/MCP client, consent process, retention limits and named human reviewer.
- Exclude DMs, private channels, cross-community profiling, autonomous sanctions, outreach, payments and contracts.
- Existing CNCF Code of Conduct jurisdiction and reporting procedures remain authoritative. Do not import incident reports into the marketplace.
- Stop on any unauthorized read/action or cross-tenant exposure. Administrators and participants can withdraw.
- Publish aggregate findings only after review. No member-identifiable screenshots or receipts.

Suggested four-week schedule after authorization: one week scope/setup, two weeks read-only review, one week aggregate evaluation and a joint stop/continue decision. Participants, dates and targets remain uncommitted.

Sources: [CNCF Code of Conduct](https://github.com/cncf/foundation/blob/main/code-of-conduct.md), [CNCF project principles](https://github.com/cncf/toc/blob/main/PRINCIPLES.md), [Slack official MCP server](https://docs.slack.dev/ai/slack-mcp-server/).
